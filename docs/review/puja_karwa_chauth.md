## Summary
Women's fasting festival observed for the longevity and well-being of husbands, primarily celebrated in North and West India.

## Significance
Karwa Chauth celebrates marital devotion and the bond between husband and wife. Women fast from sunrise to moonrise without food or water, praying for their husbands' health and long life. The fast is broken only after sighting and worshipping the moon. This vrat reflects the depth of marital commitment and mutual care.

## Vidhi (Steps)
**Step 1: Begin the Fast at Dawn**
Begin the vrat at sunrise by abstaining from all food and water. This day-long fast is observed with the intention of the husband's well-being and longevity. The fast continues until the moon is sighted in the evening.

**Step 2: Evening Preparation and Worship**
In the evening, prepare for moon worship by cleaning the area and lighting a diya. Arrange flowers and place the clay pot (karwa) prominently. This symbolizes the ritual space for the puja. Gather with other fasting women if possible, as this creates a community of shared devotion.

**Step 3: Sighting and Worshipping the Moon**
As soon as the moon becomes visible, gather to sight it. Offer flowers and water in the direction of the moon. Many women recite prayers or chants as the moon rises, invoking blessings for their husbands. The moon is believed to be a symbol of beauty, purity, and the eternal nature of love.

**Step 4: Ritual with Husband (if present) (Optional)**
If the husband is present, he applies tilaka on the wife's forehead and offers her food to break the fast. He reaffirms his commitment to her care and protection. The couple exchanges blessings and gifts. In many families, this becomes a moment of renewed commitment and affection.

**Step 5: Breaking the Fast**
After moon worship, break the fast with water or milk first, followed by a light meal. Sweets and fruits are traditionally used to break the fast. Take time to allow the body to adjust gradually after the long fast. Share the meal with family and friends to celebrate the day.

**Step 6: Health and Safety Note: Fasting Precautions**
This vrat involves a full day and evening fast without food or water until moonrise. Women with health conditions (anemia, low blood sugar, diabetes, heart conditions), pregnant mothers, nursing mothers, or those on medications should consult a doctor before observing this vrat. Do not view fasting as compulsory if health is at risk. The spirit of the ritual is devotion and love, not physical suffering.

## Regional Variations
**North India Tradition** (Regions: north)
In North India, particularly Punjab, Haryana, and Uttar Pradesh, Karwa Chauth is celebrated with great enthusiasm. Married women dress in traditional attire, apply mehndi, and gather in groups to fast together. The evening worship is more elaborate, with songs and stories celebrating marital devotion. Many families organize community gatherings for the moon-sighting ritual.
