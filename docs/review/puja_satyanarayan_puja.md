## Summary
Puja performed for family welfare, prosperity, and fulfillment of wishes. Dedicated to Lord Satyanarayan, a form of Lord Vishnu.

## Significance
Satyanarayan Puja is performed to seek the blessings of Lord Satyanarayan for family prosperity, good health, and the fulfillment of desires. It is believed that this puja removes obstacles, brings good fortune, and ensures the welfare of the entire household. The puja can be performed on any auspicious day and is often done when seeking specific blessings or thanking the deity for favors received.

## Vidhi (Steps)
**Step 1: Purification and Setup**
Clean and prepare the puja space. Arrange the deity idol or symbol of Satyanarayan on the altar. Fill the kalash with water and place it beside or in front of the idol. Light the diya.

**Step 2: Invocation and Offerings**
Offer flowers and prayers to invoke Lord Satyanarayan. Offer rice grains, flowers, and water from the kalash. Speak your intentions and wishes for your family's welfare.

**Step 3: Apply Tilak**
Apply turmeric and vermillion tilak on the idol and on yourself, marking the sacred nature of the ritual.

**Step 4: Burn Incense**
Light incense and move it in circles in front of the idol, purifying the space and creating a sacred environment.

**Step 5: Meditation and Prayers**
Sit in meditation and focus on Lord Satyanarayan. Silently offer your prayers for family prosperity, health, and the fulfillment of your righteous wishes. Recite prayers or mantras as per your tradition.

**Step 6: Prepare and Offer Halwa**
Prepare halwa (wheat flour pudding cooked in ghee and jaggery) as the main bhog offering. Offer the hot halwa at the feet of the idol along with dry fruits if available. Halwa symbolizes sweetness and prosperity.

**Step 7: Aarti**
Perform aarti by waving the lamp in front of the idol in a circular motion. The aarti signifies the completion of the puja and your gratitude to the deity.

**Step 8: Seek Blessings**
Respectfully bow to the idol and seek Satyanarayan's blessings for your family. Touch your head to receive the blessings, and then pass the blessed water from the kalash or halwa to family members.

**Step 9: Distribute Prasad**
Distribute the halwa as prasad to all family members and guests. Everyone should taste the prasad to receive the deity's blessings. Some families share the katha (story) of Satyanarayan during this time.
