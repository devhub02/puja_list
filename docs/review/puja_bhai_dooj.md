## Summary
Festival celebrating the bond between brothers and sisters. Sisters pray for their brothers' well-being and longevity by applying tilak on their foreheads.

## Significance
Bhai Dooj celebrates the sacred bond of sibling love. According to tradition, sisters apply tilak to their brothers' foreheads as a blessing for their protection and prosperity. The brother, in turn, pledges to support and protect his sister. It is a day to strengthen family bonds and express love and gratitude.

## Vidhi (Steps)
**Step 1: Prepare the Tilak**
Mix vermillion powder with rice to create a paste for the tilak. Some families add water or oil to make it bind better. Prepare this sacred mixture with devotion, as it represents blessings for the brother's well-being.

**Step 2: Light the Lamp and Offer Prayers**
Light a diya and offer flowers and water. Recite prayers for the brother's health, prosperity, and long life. Some families sing short prayers or hymns as part of the ritual. The atmosphere should be one of love and gratitude.

**Step 3: Apply the Tilak**
With prayers on the lips, apply the tilak on the brother's forehead. This mark symbolizes the sister's love and blessings for his protection and prosperity. The brother should receive this with reverence and gratitude.

**Step 4: Exchange of Gifts and Blessings**
After the tilak, the brother blesses the sister and offers gifts (sweets, dry fruits, or other tokens of love). The sister, in return, prays for the brother's well-being. Share sweets and gifts with family members to spread joy and strengthen bonds.

## Regional Variations
**Maharashtra Tradition (Bhai Tika)** (Regions: west)
In Maharashtra, this festival is known as Bhai Tika. Sisters apply a more elaborate tilak with rice, flower petals, and vermillion. The ritual often includes a more formal blessing where the sister places a hand on the brother's head while giving blessings. Gifts and sweets exchanged are more elaborate.
