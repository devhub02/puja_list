## Summary
Women's fasting festival celebrated for marital happiness and the well-being of husbands, primarily observed in North and West India.

## Significance
Hartalika Teej celebrates the love and devotion in marriage. According to tradition, Goddess Parvati fasted on this day to marry Lord Shiva. Women observe this vrat with the belief that it strengthens the marital bond and ensures the husband's long life and prosperity. The festival is marked by mehndi application, singing, and swinging.

## Vidhi (Steps)
**Step 1: Prepare with Ritual Bathing**
Begin the day with a ritual bath. Apply turmeric paste if available. This purifies the body for the day-long fast and worship that follows.

**Step 2: Fast Throughout the Day**
Observe a day-long fast without food. Water or milk may be consumed as per family tradition. Spend the day in devotional activities, singing songs, or applying mehndi with friends and family. The fast is broken the next morning after worship.

**Step 3: Evening Worship**
In the evening, prepare an altar with flowers and light a diya. Offer flowers and water to a deity (typically Goddess Parvati or a symbolic representation of marital happiness). Sing traditional Teej songs and share stories of devoted wives as per family custom.

**Step 4: Apply Mehndi**
Apply henna on hands and feet as a festive tradition. This is a joyful part of the celebration and is often done together with friends and family while singing songs.

**Step 5: Morning Worship and Breaking Fast**
On the next morning, perform worship with flowers and offerings. Pray for the husband's health and long life. After completing the prayers, break the fast with a light meal. In many regions, this is followed by celebration and the application of tilaka on the husband's forehead.

**Step 6: Health Note: Fasting Considerations**
This vrat may involve a full day fast without food. Women with health conditions, pregnant mothers, or those on medications should consult a doctor. Water may be consumed as per family tradition, which helps maintain hydration during the fast.

## Regional Variations
**North India Tradition** (Regions: north)
In North India, Hartalika Teej is celebrated with great enthusiasm. The festival is marked by women singing traditional songs (Teej geet) in groups, applying colorful mehndi designs, and swinging on swings. Many communities organize fairs where women gather to celebrate together. Sweets like gujhiya and other traditional preparations are made and distributed.
