## Summary
Women's fasting ritual observed for the well-being and long life of sons, primarily in Bihar and eastern India.

## Significance
Jitiya is a sacred vrat observed by mothers for the health and longevity of their sons. The ritual involves fasting without food or water throughout the day and night, with the fast broken only the next morning after worship. It is performed with the belief that the mother's sacrifice ensures the son's well-being.

## Vidhi (Steps)
**Step 1: Begin the Fast at Dawn**
Begin the vrat early in the morning. From this moment until the next dawn, abstain from all food and water. This rigorous fast is the core of the Jitiya vrat and is undertaken with devotion for the son's welfare.

**Step 2: Evening Prayers**
In the evening, light the diya and incense. Offer flowers and water to the deity (typically a mother goddess or child's protective deity). Recite prayers and sing traditional vrat songs as per family tradition. Maintain the fast throughout the evening.

**Step 3: Night-long Vigil**
Spend the night in prayer, meditation, or songs dedicated to the child's welfare. Some families listen to or recite stories of devoted mothers. Maintain complete fasting through the night without food or water. The spiritual devotion during this time is considered central to the vrat's power.

**Step 4: Morning Worship**
At dawn, prepare simple offerings of chickpea flour and wheat flour items. Make puri or khichdi if desired. Offer these to the deity along with flowers. Recite final prayers for the son's health and well-being. After completing the worship rituals, the fast is broken with a light meal.

**Step 5: Breaking the Fast**
After sunrise and worship, drink water or milk first to rehydrate. Then have a light meal of milk-based food, khichdi, or other easily digestible items. Avoid heavy foods immediately after the fast to allow the body to adjust gradually.

**Step 6: Health and Safety Note**
This vrat involves fasting without food or water for a full day and night. Women with health conditions (anemia, low blood pressure, diabetes), pregnant mothers, or those on medications should consult a doctor before observing this vrat. Do not push oneself beyond physical limits. The spirit of the vrat is devotion, not physical harm.

## Regional Variations
**Bihar and Jharkhand Tradition** (Regions: east)
In Bihar and Jharkhand, Jitiya is primarily observed by mothers. The vrat is rigorous and highly respected. In these regions, large groups of women often fast together, strengthening community bonds. Songs specific to Jitiya (Jitiya Geet) are sung throughout the night, creating a devotional atmosphere.
