## Summary
Worship of Goddess Lakshmi on Diwali, seeking her blessings for prosperity, wealth, and good fortune for the household.

## Significance
Lakshmi Puja during Diwali is one of the most important rituals in the Hindu calendar. Lakshmi is the goddess of wealth, prosperity, and abundance. The puja is performed to invite Lakshmi into the home and life, ensuring financial stability, success, and overall well-being of the family.

## Vidhi (Steps)
**Step 1: Purification and Setup**
Clean and decorate the puja space. Arrange the Lakshmi idol on a raised altar. Light multiple diyas around the space to symbolize the victory of light over darkness.

**Step 2: Invocation of the Goddess**
Begin the puja by offering flowers and rice at the feet of the Lakshmi idol. Invoke the goddess to bless your home with prosperity and abundance. Recite prayers or mantras as per your family tradition.

**Step 3: Apply Tilak**
Apply turmeric and vermillion tilak on the idol's forehead and on your forehead. This marks the sacred nature of the ritual and your dedication.

**Step 4: Burn Incense**
Light incense and move it in circles in front of the idol. The fragrance symbolizes the presence of the goddess and purifies the environment.

**Step 5: Meditation and Prayers**
Sit in meditation and focus on the idol. Silently offer prayers to Lakshmi for prosperity, wealth, health, and the well-being of your family. Visualize abundance flowing into your life and home.

**Step 6: Symbolic Offerings (Optional)**
Offer coins or symbolic representations of wealth at the feet of the goddess. This is a symbolic offering of your acknowledgment of her as the source of all prosperity.

**Step 7: Offer Sweets and Fruits**
Offer sweets, dry fruits, and other delicacies at the feet of the idol. Sweets symbolize the sweetness of life and prosperity.

**Step 8: Aarti**
Perform aarti by waving the lamp in front of the idol in a circular motion, expressing gratitude and completing the worship. The light of the aarti symbolizes the divine grace of the goddess.

**Step 9: Seek Blessings**
Respectfully bow to the idol and seek Lakshmi's blessings for your household. Touch the altar and then your heart as a gesture of accepting her blessings into your life.

**Step 10: Distribute Prasad**
Distribute the sweets and offerings as prasad to family members and guests. Prasad carries the blessings of the goddess and is believed to bring prosperity to all who partake in it.

## Regional Variations
**North Indian Tradition** (Regions: north)
In North India, particularly in Delhi and surrounding areas, Lakshmi Puja is often performed with the lighting of Lakshmi Chowki (sacred square) and the placement of rice grains in specific patterns. The puja may include offerings of wheat and barley.

**South Indian Tradition** (Regions: south)
In South India, Lakshmi Puja often includes the making of Kolam (decorative patterns on the ground) and is sometimes combined with the worship of other deities. The offering of banana and other local fruits is common.
