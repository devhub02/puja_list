## Summary
Four-day solar festival honoring the Sun God, primarily observed in Bihar, Jharkhand, and eastern Uttar Pradesh.

## Significance
Chhath celebrates the Sun God (Surya) as the symbol of energy and life. The ritual purifies the body and spirit through rigorous fasting. Performed primarily for the well-being and longevity of family members, especially sons.

## Vidhi (Steps)
**Step 1: Nahaay-Khaay (First Day)**
Begin the vrat with ritual bathing at a natural water source (ghat). Return home and prepare a simple meal with rice and pumpkin cooked in ghee. This breaks the fast initiated with the first sunrise observation.

**Step 2: Kharna (Second Day)**
Complete a full-day fast until sunset. At sunset, make offerings to the Sun God with fruits, sugarcane, and wheat flour items. Prepare a meal of rice pudding (kheer) and puri with jaggery.

**Step 3: Sandhya Arghya (Third Day: Evening Offering)**
Begin another day of fasting. At sunset, go to the riverbank or water source with offerings. Create a ritual platform with coconut, banana, sugarcane, and wheat-flour items arranged on woven baskets. Offer water to the setting Sun while singing traditional songs. This continues the fast overnight.

**Step 4: Usha Arghya (Fourth Day: Dawn Offering)**
After fasting through the night, offer water and prayers to the rising Sun at dawn. Use the same offerings as the evening ritual. After completing the arghya, conclude the fast with a simple meal of rice, lentils, and seasonal vegetables. The vrat is now complete.

**Step 5: Safety Note: Ghat Safety**
As the puja involves standing in water at dawn and dusk, take precautions: watch water depth and current, avoid slippery steps, supervise children closely, be aware of cold water temperature, and have assistance available. Do not perform this ritual alone.

**Step 6: Health Note: Fasting Safety**
Chhath involves long fasts without food and sometimes without water. People with health conditions (diabetes, heart disease, anemia), pregnant or nursing mothers, and those on medications should consult a doctor before beginning this vrat. Do not treat fasting as compulsory if health is at risk.

## Regional Variations
**Bihar and Jharkhand Tradition** (Regions: east)
In Bihar and Jharkhand, Chhath is celebrated with great fervor. Large processions of worshippers go to the ghats, and the offerings are often more elaborate with extensive fruit and flower arrangements. Songs specific to the region (Chhath Geet) are sung throughout the festival. Many households use bamboo baskets for offerings.

**Eastern Uttar Pradesh Practice** (Regions: north)
In eastern Uttar Pradesh and parts of Nepal Terai, Chhath is observed with similar intensity but with slight variations in rituals and offerings. Some families make specific khichdi (rice and lentil preparation) instead of kheer during Kharna. The timing and sequence of rituals may vary based on local customs and panchang (lunar calendar).
