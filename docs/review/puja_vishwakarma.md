## Summary
Festival honoring Vishwakarma, the divine architect and craftsman. Widely celebrated in industries, workshops, and by artisans and engineers.

## Significance
Vishwakarma Puja celebrates the patron deity of artisans, engineers, and craftspeople. He is worshipped for skill, knowledge, and protection in one's profession. The puja typically includes blessing tools and machinery, invoking safety and prosperity in work.

## Vidhi (Steps)
**Step 1: Prepare the Workspace**
Clean and prepare the workshop or workspace where tools and machinery are located. Arrange all tools and equipment in an organized manner. This place becomes the shrine for the puja.

**Step 2: Set up the Altar**
Create a small altar or sacred space. Place an idol or image of Vishwakarma if available, or simply use flowers and incense to mark the sacred space. Arrange water, flowers, and offering items.

**Step 3: Invoke Vishwakarma**
Light the diya and incense. Offer flowers and water to the deity. Recite prayers invoking Vishwakarma for his blessings. The specific prayers vary by family tradition and region—follow your own or consult a local guide.

**Step 4: Bless the Tools and Machinery**
The main ritual involves blessing all tools and machinery. Circle the tools with offerings or simply touch them with flowers and tilaka while reciting blessings. This symbolizes invoking protection and prosperity for work performed with these tools.

**Step 5: Distribution of Blessed Items**
Distribute blessed water, flowers, or sweets among workers, colleagues, or family members. This marks the completion of the puja and spreads the blessings. Traditionally, workers take the day off to celebrate together.

**Step 6: Safety Note: Tool and Machinery Precautions**
During the puja, ensure all machinery is turned off and safe. Do not operate any equipment during the ritual. Keep the area clear of hazards. If the puja involves blessing tools near running machinery, ensure proper safety protocols are followed and machines are isolated from the ritual area.

## Regional Variations
**Factory and Industrial Tradition** (Regions: pan_india)
In factories and large industries, Vishwakarma Puja is celebrated on a large scale. The ritual often includes blessing all machinery on the factory floor, group prayers for safety, and distribution of sweets among all workers. Many organizations declare a holiday to allow workers to participate in the celebrations.
