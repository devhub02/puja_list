## Summary
Worship of Lord Hanuman, the devoted follower of Lord Rama, seeking his blessings for strength, courage, protection, and spiritual growth. Hanuman is one of the most widely worshipped deities in Hinduism.

## Significance
Hanuman Puja celebrates the strength, courage, loyalty, and devotion of Lord Hanuman. Hanuman is worshipped as the embodiment of discipline, righteousness, and selfless service. He is believed to be the greatest devotee of Lord Rama and his life exemplifies dedication to the divine. Devotees seek Hanuman's blessings for physical strength, mental clarity, protection from negative forces, and spiritual enlightenment.

## Vidhi (Steps)
**Step 1: Purification and Setup**
Clean and prepare the puja space. Arrange the Hanuman idol on the altar. Decorate with red cloth or flowers as red is the sacred color for Hanuman. Light diyas around the altar. Sit in a comfortable position facing the idol.

**Step 2: Apply Tilak**
Apply turmeric and vermillion tilak on your forehead and on the idol. Vermillion is particularly sacred to Hanuman, so it is applied generously on his form.

**Step 3: Burn Incense**
Light incense and move it in circles in front of the idol. The fragrance purifies the space and creates a sacred environment for worship.

**Step 4: Offer Flowers**
Offer marigold and other flowers at the feet of Hanuman. Flowers represent devotion and beauty. The offering of flowers is a traditional way to honor the deity.

**Step 5: Meditation and Prayers**
Sit in quiet meditation and focus on Hanuman. Visualize his divine form—powerful, courageous, and devoted. Silently offer prayers seeking his blessings for strength, protection, courage, and spiritual guidance. Recite the Hanuman Chalisa or other prayers as per your tradition.

**Step 6: Offer Rice and Vermillion**
Offer rice grains mixed with vermillion at the feet of the idol. This is a traditional offering that symbolizes respect and devotion to Hanuman.

**Step 7: Offer Sweets**
Offer sweets or laddoos at the feet of Hanuman as a mark of devotion and celebration. Sweets symbolize the sweetness of divine presence and blessing.

**Step 8: Aarti**
Perform aarti by waving the lamp in front of the idol in a circular motion. The aarti expresses gratitude to Hanuman for his strength, protection, and guidance.

**Step 9: Seek Blessings**
Respectfully bow to the idol and seek Hanuman's blessings. Touch your forehead to show complete dedication and receive his grace. Ask for strength of body and mind, courage to face challenges, and protection from harm.

**Step 10: Distribute Prasad**
Distribute the offered sweets as prasad to family members and guests. Prasad carries Hanuman's blessings and is believed to grant strength and protection to all who partake. The distribution is an important part of completing the puja.

## Regional Variations
**Tuesday Worship** (Regions: pan_india)
Tuesday is considered the most auspicious day for Hanuman Puja, as it is believed to be associated with Hanuman's powers. Many devotees observe a special puja on Tuesdays, often fasting or performing additional rituals. Hanuman Puja on Tuesday is believed to bring greater benefits and blessings.

**Hanuman Chalisa Recitation** (Regions: pan_india)
Many devotees recite the Hanuman Chalisa (40 verses in praise of Hanuman) during the puja. The Chalisa is a powerful devotional prayer that praises Hanuman's qualities and seeks his blessings. Reciting the Chalisa is believed to bring protection and strength.
