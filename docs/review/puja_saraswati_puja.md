## Summary
Worship of Goddess Saraswati, the deity of knowledge, learning, and the arts. Seeks her blessings for intellectual growth and academic success.

## Significance
Saraswati Puja celebrates Goddess Saraswati, the embodiment of wisdom, learning, and creativity. The puja is particularly important for students, scholars, artists, and teachers. It marks the beginning of spring and is believed to enhance knowledge, creativity, and intellectual abilities.

## Vidhi (Steps)
**Step 1: Purification and Setup**
Clean the puja space and decorate it with yellow flowers. Arrange the Saraswati idol, books, and musical instruments on the altar. Light the diya to symbolize the illumination of knowledge.

**Step 2: Invocation of the Goddess**
Begin by offering yellow flowers and rice grains at the feet of the Saraswati idol. Invoke the goddess to bless you with knowledge, wisdom, and creative abilities. Recite prayers or mantras as per your tradition.

**Step 3: Apply Tilak**
Apply turmeric and vermillion tilak on the idol and on yourself. This marks the sacred nature of the ritual and your dedication to learning.

**Step 4: Burn Incense**
Light incense and move it in circles in front of the idol. The fragrance is believed to enhance clarity of thought and concentration.

**Step 5: Meditation and Prayers**
Sit in quiet meditation and focus on Saraswati. Silently offer prayers for intellectual growth, clarity of mind, and the ability to pursue your chosen field of knowledge or art. Visualize yourself gaining wisdom and achieving your academic or creative goals.

**Step 6: Offer Books and Instruments**
Place books and musical instruments at the feet of the idol as symbolic offerings. Books represent the pursuit of knowledge and instruments represent creativity and the arts. Some families touch their pens and educational materials to the altar for blessings.

**Step 7: Aarti**
Perform aarti by waving the lamp in front of the idol in a circular motion. The light of the aarti represents the dispelling of ignorance and the illumination of knowledge.

**Step 8: Seek Blessings**
Respectfully bow to the idol and seek Saraswati's blessings for academic success, intellectual growth, and creative excellence. Touch your head to the altar to receive her blessings.

**Step 9: Distribute Prasad**
Distribute the offered sweets and other items as prasad to family members and guests, particularly students. Prasad carries Saraswati's blessings for knowledge and wisdom.

## Regional Variations
**Eastern India Tradition** (Regions: east)
In Eastern India, particularly in Bengal, Saraswati Puja is a major festival with grand celebrations. Yellow flowers (especially marigold) are extensively used, and the puja is often performed with elaborate rituals and community involvement. Students place their books and pens on the altar for blessings.
